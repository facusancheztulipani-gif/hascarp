Delivery policy

Digital delivery only. Nothing is posted: no carrier, no shipping charge and no parcel to wait for.

What you receive

PDF files that you can read on a phone, tablet or computer, or print at home. There is no physical product at any price.

When

Access is granted as soon as the payment is authorised, normally within one minute.

How

Two ways, both automatic: a download link on the order confirmation page, and an email to the address given at checkout with the same links. Bonus volumes arrive with the main title.

How long you keep access

Access does not expire. You can download the files again from the links in your confirmation email, unless the order has been refunded.

If it does not arrive

First check your promotions and spam folders. Then email librodigital365@gmail.com with the address you used at checkout. The usual causes are a filtered email or a typo in the address, and we resend the files directly, usually the same day.
