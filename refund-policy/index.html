Refund policy

Every title comes with a money-back guarantee, counted in days from the date of purchase.

How long you have

  • Bread baking titles, including add-ons: 60 days from the date of purchase.
  • Drawing titles: 30 days from the date of purchase.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@hascarp.shop from the address you used at checkout, or reply to your confirmation email, and say you want a refund. Add the order number if you have it; if not, your email address is enough. No form, no phone call and no reason needed.

How long it takes

We reply within one business day and send the refund to your original payment method. Your bank then takes about five to ten business days to show it.

What happens to your access

Nothing is shipped, so there is nothing to send back. The refund closes that order, and its download links can stop working. You do not need to prove you deleted anything.

Add-on titles

Each add-on is a separate charge, so it can be refunded on its own without touching the rest of your order.

If you see two charges

A second charge is usually an add-on accepted right after checkout. Write to us and we will tell you what each one is. A real duplicate is refunded in full.

Same policy everywhere

This policy applies in the same way on every storefront and in every language. It is in addition to any right your local consumer law gives you.
