Payments & security

Card details are entered on the payment provider's hosted form, not on ours.

Processing

Payments are processed by PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment providers. Our storefronts receive the result of the authorisation, never the card number.

Encryption

Every storefront and checkout page is served over HTTPS with TLS, and HTTP requests are redirected to HTTPS.

Authentication

European cards are authenticated with 3-D Secure where the issuer requires it.

How the charge appears

Purchases appear on your card statement as HASCARP.SHOP, the address of this website. If you do not recognise a charge, write to support@hascarp.shop before contacting your bank.

Payment methods

Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Apple Pay, Google Pay and other methods appear at checkout when they are available for your device and country. What you see at checkout is what we accept for your order.
