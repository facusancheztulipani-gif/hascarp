The business

One sentence for the reviewer: we sell our own instructional PDFs and video lessons, each a one-time purchase, delivered by download and email within a minute of payment. Nothing is shipped and nothing recurs.

HAS CARP LLC writes, produces and publishes instructional material for people who want to learn a hands-on craft at home. Each title is written for one subject and one language market, and is sold directly on the brand's own website, listed under What we sell.

A typical purchase is a PDF book of 40 or more step-by-step recipes or exercises, with printable reference charts and a set of bonus titles included. Some titles add video lessons. The buyer pays once, downloads the files immediately, and keeps them. There is no membership, no subscription, no auto-renewal, and no physical shipment at any price point.

We publish in German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Slovak and Czech. Buyers are consumers, mostly between 35 and 70, who find a title through social media advertising and buy on the same visit. All customer-facing text, support and refunds are handled in the buyer's own language.

What we sell

Eight storefronts, two product categories. Every title is a digital download. This list is the complete catalogue; each new title is added here on the day it goes on sale.

Home bread baking, including gluten-free

Books that teach bread baking as a repeatable process rather than by feel: hydration and binder tables, proofing times by room temperature, oven and bread-machine settings, and more than 40 step-by-step recipes per title. The gluten-free titles are built around a binder ratio table for people who cannot eat wheat.

Prices and currencies

Every price on every storefront is shown with its currency written out next to the figure, and that figure is what the card is charged.

  • Price range. Single titles run from EUR 9.99 to EUR 19.99, and bundles of a main title with its bonus volumes up to EUR 39.98. Where a storefront charges in US dollars the same titles are USD 19.99, and bundles up to USD 43.98. The Czech storefront prices in Czech koruna, from CZK 149 to CZK 329.
  • Currency per market. Each storefront charges in one currency, listed in the tables above. The buyer sees that currency, with the three-letter code or the local symbol, before paying and on the confirmation page.
  • Taxes. European prices are shown VAT inclusive. The buyer is never charged more than the figure displayed.
  • One-time payment. Every price is a single charge for permanent access. We do not run subscriptions, trials, free-to-paid conversions, or any charge after the first one.
  • Promotional pricing. Launch and half-price promotions run for a limited period and are shown as the promotional price next to the struck-through regular price. The promotional price is what is charged. Bonus volumes advertised as included are delivered with the main title at no extra cost, and the offer terms sit next to the buy button.

Delivery

Digital delivery only. Nothing is posted, so there are no shipping charges, no delivery addresses and no carriers.

  • When. Access is granted as soon as the payment is authorised, normally within one minute.
  • How. Two ways, both automatic: a download link on the order confirmation page, and an email to the address given at checkout containing the same links. Bonus volumes arrive in the same email as the main title.
  • What arrives. PDF files, readable on a phone, tablet or computer, plus video lessons on the titles that include them. Files may be downloaded again at any time from the links in the confirmation email.
  • Access period. Permanent. Access does not expire and is not revoked.
  • If it does not arrive. Email support@hascarp.shop with the email address used at checkout and we resend the files directly, usually the same day. The most common cause is the confirmation email sitting in a promotions or spam folder, or a typo in the address at checkout; both are fixed by a resend.

Refunds and cancellations

Every title carries a money-back guarantee. We refund on request, without asking the buyer to justify it.

Guarantee period

  • Bread baking titles: 60 days from purchase.
  • Drawing titles: 30 days from purchase.

How to request one

Email support@hascarp.shop from the address used at checkout, or reply to the confirmation email, and write that you want a refund. No form, no phone call and no reason required. We reply within one business day and process the refund to the original payment method; the bank then takes five to ten business days to show it.

Cancellations

There is nothing to cancel. All purchases are one-time payments, there are no subscriptions or recurring charges, and no payment instrument is stored for future use.

Returns

No goods are shipped, so there is no return process. A refund closes the matter; the buyer keeps the downloaded files.

Disputes

If a charge looks unfamiliar, email support@hascarp.shop before contacting the bank: the charge descriptor shows the storefront's brand name, which is not always the name the buyer remembers. We would rather refund directly than have a buyer wait for a dispute to resolve.

Privacy policy

We collect what an order needs and what the storefront needs to work. We do not sell personal data, and we never see or store card numbers.

What we collect

  • Order data: name, email address, country, the titles purchased, the amount and currency paid, and the time of the order.
  • Payment data: entered on the payment provider's own hosted form. We receive confirmation of the result, the card brand and the last four digits. The full card number never reaches our systems.
  • Site data: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and the advertisement a visit came from, through Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity. Used to measure advertising and to see where a page confuses people.
  • Support data: the contents of emails sent to us.

Why, and on what basis

Order and payment data are processed to perform the sales contract and to meet US tax and accounting obligations. Site analytics and advertising measurement are processed with consent where consent is required, which EU and UK visitors give or refuse in the cookie banner on each storefront. Support data is processed on our legitimate interest in answering the customer.

Who else processes it

Our payment providers, our email delivery provider, our storefront and hosting provider, and the analytics providers named above. Each receives only the data it needs to perform its function, and none is permitted to use it for its own purposes.

How long we keep it

Order records for seven years, as US tax rules require. Support email for two years. Analytics data for the retention period of the provider, which does not exceed 25 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@hascarp.shop from the address on the order; we verify the request and answer within 30 days. We honour the rights given by the GDPR in the EU and UK, and by the CCPA in California, for every buyer wherever they live. Deletion does not remove the order record itself where tax law requires us to keep it.

Children

Our products are for adults. We do not knowingly sell to or collect data from anyone under 18.

Payments and security

Card data is entered on the payment provider's hosted form, not on ours, and is handled under the PCI Data Security Standard.

  • Processing. Card payments are processed by PCI DSS Level 1 certified providers, including Stripe, Inc. Our storefronts receive the result of the authorisation, never the card number.
  • Encryption. Every storefront and every checkout page is served over HTTPS with TLS, and HTTP requests are redirected to HTTPS.
  • Authentication. European cards are authenticated with 3-D Secure where the issuer requires it. Administrative access to our storefronts and payment accounts requires two-factor authentication.
  • Descriptor. The charge on a buyer's statement shows the storefront brand they bought from, so it can be recognised on the bill.
