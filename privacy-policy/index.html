Privacy policy

We collect what an order needs and what a storefront needs to work. We do not sell personal data, and we never see or store card numbers.

Who is responsible

HAS CARP LLC, 1057 NW 136th Ave, Miami, FL 33182, United States. Contact: librodigital365@gmail.com. This policy covers this website and every storefront listed in our catalogue.

What we collect

  • Order data: name, email address, country, the titles purchased, the amount and currency paid, and the time of the order.
  • Payment data: entered on the payment provider's own hosted form. We receive the result, the card brand and the last four digits. The full card number never reaches our systems.
  • Delivery records: when the files were accessed, with the IP address and browser used, to confirm delivery and resolve disputes.
  • Site data: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and the advertisement a visit came from.
  • Support data: the contents of the emails you send us.

Why, and on what basis

Order and payment data are processed to perform the sales contract and to meet US tax and accounting obligations. Site analytics and advertising measurement are processed with your consent where consent is required. Support data is processed on our legitimate interest in answering you.

Pixels and cookies

This website, hascarp.shop, uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising pixels. Our storefronts use the Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity to measure advertising and to see where a page confuses people. EU and UK visitors can accept or refuse them in the storefront's cookie banner. We do not send health data or other sensitive information to these tools.

Who we share data with

Our payment processors, our email delivery provider, our storefront and hosting provider, and the analytics providers named above. Each one receives only the data it needs for its function, and none may use it for its own purposes.

How long we keep it

Order records for seven years, as US tax rules require. Support email for two years. Analytics data for the provider's retention period, which does not exceed 25 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email librodigital365@gmail.com from the address on the order; we verify the request and answer within 30 days. We honour the rights given by the GDPR in the EU and UK and by the CCPA in California, for every buyer wherever they live. To delete your data, follow the steps on the data deletion page.

Children

Our products are for adults. We do not knowingly sell to or collect data from anyone under 18.
