Data deletion

How to have your personal data deleted.

How to ask

Email librodigital365@gmail.com from the address on your order, with the subject line "Delete my data". If you no longer use that address, tell us which one it was and we will agree a way to confirm it is you.

What we delete

Your customer profile, support emails, and the personal data linked to your visits on our storefronts. If you are on a mailing list, you are removed from it.

What we have to keep

The order record itself, for seven years, because US tax rules require it. It is kept only for that purpose. Requests for deletion do not affect any refund you are entitled to.

How long it takes

We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.
