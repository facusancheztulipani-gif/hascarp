What the book is

A PDF recipe book of gluten-free, sugar-free and lactose-free baking: breads, sandwich loaves, baguettes, pizza and focaccia, croissants, cakes and homemade pasta, organised by category, with ingredients weighed in grams and oven temperatures for every recipe. Made with gluten-free flour from an ordinary supermarket and no special equipment.
